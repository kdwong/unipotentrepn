Lowest harmonic chains and painted bipartitions Local computation

Let G = SO(n,n+1). This program computes all theta lift chains that gives a special unipotent representation of G with lowest harmonics equal to a fine K-type of highest weight (1…10…0 | 0…0). For each chain, it also computes its corresponding painted bipartition.
